Lightlike

Lightlike is a Lean 4 library of the mathematics of fundamental physics, built on Mathlib and Physlib: definitions, proofs checked by Lean's kernel, and open problems stated exactly. So far it covers mostly spin-foam and state-sum quantum gravity, and the general relativity, representation theory, PL topology and Regge calculus they rest on. Each entry of the catalog cites the source of its statement and says what it does not cover.
