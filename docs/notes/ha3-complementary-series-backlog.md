# HA3, t3: what the Casimir argument leaves open

Result: for the principal-series model `|cx+d|^{-τ-1}` of SL(2,ℝ), no positive definite sesquilinear form on smooth functions
makes `E, F, H` skew when `|τ| > 1` (`no_invariant_form`, `no_invariant_form_smooth`) or when `τ = 1`
(`no_invariant_form_smooth_boundary`). The statements are infinitesimal (generators on smooth vectors), not group-level.

Backlog (not claimed):

1. **τ = −1.** The Casimir eigenvalue is 0 and the constant function is an invariant vector (`E v = F v = H v = 0`), so
   the `K`-fixed vector gives no contradiction. A different argument is needed to decide whether a positive definite
   skew form exists on all smooth functions at `τ = −1` (the model is the action on functions on the projective line by
   composition; `f ↦ f'` intertwines the quotient by the constants with the `τ = 1` model).
2. **Group-level statement.** To pass from a unitary representation of the group to skewness of `E, F, H` on smooth vectors,
   a topology on the smooth vectors in which the form is continuous is needed (a unitary representation on a Hilbert space
   has one; an abstract sesquilinear form does not).
3. **Sign of the parameter.** `τ ↦ −τ` with `|τ| < 1` gives the equivalent unitary complementary series: nothing to prove.
