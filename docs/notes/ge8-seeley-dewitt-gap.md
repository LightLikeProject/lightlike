# GE8: what is missing for the Seeley-DeWitt expansion (G1-018 stays partial)

On main: `HasHeatExpansion`, coefficient uniqueness, the `S²` trace `1/t + 1/3 + t/15 + O(t²)`, the flat torus with no `t⁰` term (all further coefficients zero), `S³`, products, and `a₁` tied to Lightlike's scalar curvature on the spheres (`SphereCurvatureTie`).
Missing: the existence theorem on a general compact Riemannian manifold: `Tr e^{-tD} ∼ Σ a_k t^{k-n/2}` for a Laplace-type operator `D`, with `a₁ = (4π)^{-n/2} ∫ R/6`.
Nothing constructs the expansion from the geometry: the heat parametrix, Duhamel iteration and the local coefficient recursion are not formalised.
The examples verify the statement on spaces whose spectrum is known; they do not prove it in general. Estimated well above the $40 cap.
Status (24 Nov 2026): accepted as partial, the general theorem not met.
