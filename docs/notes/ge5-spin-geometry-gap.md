# GE5: what is missing for the Dirac operator on a spin manifold (G1-017 stays partial)

On main: the local Lichnerowicz formula in a frame on ℝⁿ with a Clifford representation, the Friedrich bound `λ² ≥ n²/4` for compactly supported eigenspinors in the sphere chart, the `S²` eigenspinor equation in the chart, and the Connes distance on ℝⁿ and the circle.
Missing: spin structures and the spinor bundle on a manifold, and the global formula `D² = ∇*∇ + R/4` (Lichnerowicz) on a spin manifold.
Missing: the Dirac spectrum of the unit `S²` as `±(k+1)` with multiplicity `2(k+1)` and its completeness, and the check that the formula with `R/2` fails.
Global spin geometry (spin lifts of the frame bundle, the spin connection on a bundle) is absent upstream; estimated above the $40 cap.
Status (24 Nov 2026): accepted as partial, the general theorem not met.
