# Audit of the "main result" flag in the results catalogue

Audit table only. Nothing in the catalogue changes until the maintainers approve this table.

## Test and method

Each of the 263 entries of `docs/results.yaml` was tested as follows.

* (a) Named theorem: the result has a standard name used in at least one textbook or survey; the evidence column gives the place that was read.
* (b) Main theorem of its source: the abstract or introduction of the cited source names the result as its main theorem; the sentence is quoted.
* Anything else is not main: lemmas, special cases, corollaries, definitions, partial versions. A result is main only if the entry proves the named or main theorem itself; a special case or one clause of it is `none` even when the name matches.
* `unverified`: the book or paper that would settle it could not be read; the reason is given and the maintainers decide. Unverified entries keep their current flag in the proposal.

What was read: the abstracts of all 58 arXiv sources cited by the catalogue (from the arXiv API) and the full texts of Lickorish, Roberts, Klazar, Krajewski, Kar–Sengupta, Carroll, Gourgoulhon, Dittrich–Freidel–Speziale, Derezinski, Faulhuber, Mukhametzhanov–Zhiboedov, Kamiński et al., Engle–Pereira, Teschl, Gurau, Barrett et al. and Ambjørn–Jurkiewicz–Loll at the places quoted. The books cited by the catalogue (Hörmander, Edmonds, Varshalovich, Knapp, Villani, Moise, McCleary, Hall, Lee, Penrose–Rindler) could not be read; where a chapter title was confirmed from a publisher record it is said so. For `none` entries without a longer remark, the judgement rests on the title, the entry note, the label of the cited location and the abstract of the source; no standard name for the statement was found.
